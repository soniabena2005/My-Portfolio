About me

I am a fourth-year engineering student specializing in System and Security at the National Higher School of Advanced Technologies (ENSTA) in Algiers, Algeria. I am passionate about technology and enjoy learning new tools and building applications that solve real problems.

Alongside my studies, I design and develop web and mobile projects: UniHub, a platform for university students built with React and Firebase, and Planivia, a life-planning app built with Flutter. These projects taught me to work in a team, structure a codebase, and deliver features from idea to working product.

My goal is to become a skilled engineer and contribute to innovative projects that have a positive impact on society. I am currently open to internships and collaboration opportunities.

My projects

UniHub

UniHub is a centralized platform made by students, for students. It makes university life easier, more connected, and more organized by bringing several useful services together in one place.

It includes study resources, a lost and found system, an events board, and a private chat system for communication between students.

My contribution: Private chat system

Planivia

Planivia is a life-planning and organization app developed with Flutter by me and a friend. It helps users organize their daily tasks, goals, activities, and personal life in one place.

It includes a to-do list, weekly, monthly, and yearly goals, a shopping and grocery list, a calendar for important events, and a journal.
